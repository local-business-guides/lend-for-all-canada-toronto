What to Expect When You Compare Loans in Toronto

Start with what the money is for and about how much you need. That keeps the search focused. From there, we help you line up options that fit your situation.

When you look at an offer, check the total you'd repay, the length of the term, and how often payments come out of your account. Fees matter too, so ask what they are and when they apply. If a detail isn't clear, ask for it in writing. A good lender won't mind the question.

What to look at before you choose

No matter which option you're considering, a few questions are worth asking:

  • What is the total amount I'll repay?
  • How long is the term, and how often are payments due?
  • Are there fees, and when are they charged?
  • What happens if I miss a payment or want to pay early?

These answers tell you far more than a single monthly figure does. If a lender can't explain something clearly, that's worth noting.
