Questions to ask a credit counseling service in Toronto

Counseling providers aren't all set up the same way. Before you share your finances, get clear answers to a few things.

  • Is this a nonprofit, or a business that earns money from what it sells me?
  • Will you tell me about every fee before I sign anything?
  • Do you look at my whole budget, or jump straight to one product?
  • What happens with my creditors if I start a plan?
  • Can I just talk first, with no commitment?

A straight answer to each of these is what you want. If you feel pushed toward a decision on the first call, slow down.

What to prepare before your first conversation

You don't need a perfect spreadsheet. You just need honest numbers. Gather what you can.

  • Every debt you have: credit cards, lines of credit, car loans, anything overdue
  • The balance, interest rate and minimum payment on each
  • Your take-home income, including side work
  • Your regular monthly costs, like rent, groceries, transit and phone
  • Any letters or calls from collectors

If you can't find everything, bring what you've got. A counselor can help fill in the gaps.

Mistakes people make when they look for credit counseling

Most of these come from stress, not carelessness.

  • Waiting too long. The earlier you talk to someone, the more choices you usually have.
  • Leaving debts out. A counselor can only plan around what they see, so share all of it.
  • Believing anyone who promises to wipe out your debt. Be wary of big claims and pressure.
  • Taking a new loan before you understand the plan. Sometimes borrowing helps. Sometimes it just moves the problem.
  • Ignoring creditor calls. It rarely makes them stop and it can make things harder.

When to call a credit counseling service

Call when the monthly math isn't working. Maybe you're paying one card with another, or the minimums eat most of your paycheque. Maybe you've missed a payment and feel dread whenever the phone rings. Any of those is a good enough reason.

Questions people ask about credit counseling service Toronto

Do I need all my paperwork before I call a credit counseling service?

No. It helps to have your debts, income and monthly costs on hand, but you can start with what you know. A counselor can help you work out the rest.

Will credit counseling always lead to a new loan?

No. Sometimes a budget and a payment plan are enough. At Lend for All Canada we'll only point you toward a loan if it makes sense for your situation.

How do I avoid a bad credit counseling provider?

Ask about fees and how they're paid before you share details. Be cautious if someone promises to erase your debt or pushes you to sign on the first call.
